When Was Your Last Eye Exam?

Eye Exams for Adults in Pine Beach, Without the Rush

Book AppointmentCall: (732) 978-9306

With work, family and everything else on your plate, an eye exam is easy to push down the list—especially when your eyes feel fine. At Academy Vision, adult exams are unhurried. Our optometrists take the time to listen, explain what they find, and make sure you feel comfortable, so bring your questions to our Pine Beach office.

Feeling Fine Isn’t the Same as a Checkup

Plenty of adults go years between eye exams. Glasses that are slightly off, eyes that ache after a long day of screens, or headlights that seem harsher at night can creep in so slowly that they start to feel normal. In your 40s, you may also notice that menus and phone screens are harder to read up close.

A quick vision screening or an online test can check how clearly you see letters. Neither can tell you how healthy your eyes are, and passing one doesn’t mean everything is fine. Some conditions, including glaucoma, can develop gradually without symptoms you’d notice.

What a Full Adult Exam Gives You

  • A thorough exam, not a quick check. Academy Vision provides thorough eye exams, so you can feel confident about your vision.
  • Your prescription and your eye health in one visit. Your exam is designed to give you a complete picture of how you see and how healthy your eyes are.
  • Continuity over time. We monitor your eye health closely over the years, which makes slow changes easier to catch early.

Your Exam, Step by Step

Every visit is a little different, but here’s the general order of things.

  1. We listen first. Tell us how your eyes feel at work, behind the wheel and on screens, and mention any health conditions or medications.
  2. We check your prescription to make sure you’re seeing clearly.
  3. We evaluate how your eyes are working together, which matters for reading, screen time and driving.
  4. We take a close look at your eye health, including any signs of concern, since many conditions don’t cause symptoms early on.
  5. We talk through anything we find in plain language, so you know what it means and what comes next.

A Pine Beach Practice with Deep Roots

Academy Vision was founded in 1984 by Dr. Robert Ullman, and for over 40 years, families in and around Pine Beach have trusted us with their vision. It’s not unusual for us to see several generations of one family in the same week.

That long view matters at an adult exam. Families throughout Pine Beach, Beachwood and Toms River come back year after year, and seeing the same practice over time makes it easier to notice what has changed and what hasn’t.

A Prescription Check Is Only Part of It

A fast prescription check answers one question: do you need new lenses? A comprehensive exam also asks whether your eyes are healthy, whether they’re working well together, and whether anything has shifted since last time. If you wear contacts, mention it when you book, because a contact lens exam goes a step beyond a standard eye exam.

Booking for the Whole Household?

If you’re due for an exam, the rest of your family may be too. If it’s easier to schedule everyone together, we can do that. We see patients at every stage of life, so you can also read about children’s eye care and senior eye exams, or start with our overview of comprehensive eye exams.

How often should adults have an eye exam?

It depends on your age, your eye health and your history. People with diabetes, a family history of glaucoma, or a prescription that keeps changing may need to come in more often. Ask your optometrist at the end of your visit when to return, and if your vision changes before then, don’t wait.

What should I bring to my appointment?

Bring your current glasses, your contacts or the boxes they came in, the names and doses of your medications, and all of your insurance cards. If there are questions you don’t want to forget, jot them down too—there’s time to ask them.

Will insurance cover my exam?

Your plan, and why you’re coming in, both make a difference. Vision insurance is typically used for routine care, while medical insurance comes into play when there’s a health-related concern with your eyes. Our team at Academy Vision will help you sort out your benefits and explain what’s covered, and you can see the plans we accept before you call.
