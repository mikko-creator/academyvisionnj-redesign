Growing Eyes, Gentle Care

Looking After Your Child’s Eyes in Pine Beach as They Grow

Book AppointmentCall: (732) 978-9306

A first eye exam, glasses for the school year, a prescription that keeps changing—your child’s vision needs can shift a lot as they grow. At Academy Vision, we take a gentle approach that helps kids feel comfortable from the moment they come through the door, and we’re here for every stage along the way. Call (732) 978-9306 or book online to schedule your child’s visit in Pine Beach.

Explore Care for Kids

  • Pediatric Eye Exams: a look at how sharply your child sees near and far, how their eyes focus and team up, and how healthy their eyes are.
  • Back-to-School Eye Exams: get a full exam in before classes begin, since school screenings check far less.
  • Myopia Management: options designed to slow progression when a nearsighted prescription keeps climbing, including ortho-k and soft contact lenses made for kids.
  • Contact Lenses for Kids: what parents should know about readiness, safety and fittings.
  • Kids’ Eyewear: lightweight, durable frames suited to school, play and everyday wear.

Why It Helps to Stay with One Practice

Children’s eyes change as they grow, and kids often don’t mention that something looks different. When the same practice sees your child year after year, each exam can be compared with the last, which makes it easier to spot a prescription that’s creeping up, a focusing problem that’s starting to affect homework, or a change in eye health.

For school-age children, the American Optometric Association recommends a comprehensive eye exam every year, or more often when the eye doctor advises it. If your child’s vision has been changing, it’s worth asking our eye doctors about options such as Stellest® lenses or ortho-k. Dr. Tyler Lesko’s clinical focus includes pediatrics and myopia management, using orthokeratology and MiSight lenses.

A Local Practice Families Know

Since Dr. Robert Ullman founded Academy Vision in 1984, families in and around Pine Beach have trusted us with their vision for over 40 years. Many come back year after year from Pine Beach, Beachwood and Toms River, and it’s not unusual for us to see several generations of the same family in one week.

You’ll find us on Route 9 near the Mizzen Avenue light, in a standalone building with its own parking lot, which helps when you’re juggling car seats, backpacks and siblings.

Insurance and Booking

Vision insurance is typically used for routine care. Our team will help you sort out your child’s benefits, explain what’s covered and make the most of your plan. If it’s easier to schedule your children together, we can do that too.

What signs might mean my child needs an eye exam?

Watch for squinting, sitting very close to screens or books, frequent eye rubbing, headaches, covering one eye, tilting the head, or avoiding reading and close-up work. Any of these is a good reason to book a visit.

Do you have frames for kids?

We offer kids’ frames that are lightweight, flexible and designed to stay comfortable throughout the day, so they can keep up with school and play.

Will my child be nervous?

Some kids are, and that’s okay. We take a gentle approach and keep everything age-appropriate and easy to follow, so your child knows what’s happening at each step.
