Let’s Find Glasses You Love

Eyeglass Frames & Lenses in Pine Beach

Book Appointment

Finding the right pair of glasses shouldn’t feel like a hassle. Whether you need something for everyday wear, work, or just a fresh new look, we’re here to make the process simple. At our Pine Beach optical, we help you find glasses that feel good and fit your life, without overcomplicating things. Visit Academy Vision for your next pair.

A Better Eyewear Experience

Choosing glasses isn’t just about picking a frame off the shelf. Our Pine Beach opticians take the time to understand how you use your eyes daily, whether you’re working on a screen, driving, reading, or always on the go. From there, we guide you toward options that fit your vision, comfort, and style. No pressure, no guesswork—just help that makes sense.

Same-Day Glasses Available

Sometimes you don’t want to wait days or weeks for your new glasses. With our in-office finishing lab in Pine Beach, many prescriptions can be made the very same day. That means you can come in for your eye exam, pick out your frames, and leave with your new glasses in hand. It’s quick, convenient, and one less thing to worry about.

Frames for Adults and Kids

We carry a wide selection of frames for every age and style. Whether you’re looking for something classic, modern, or a little bolder, we’ll help you find a pair that feels like you. And if you’re bringing your kids in, we make it easy to find comfortable, durable options.

Kids’ Frames That Fit Their Life

Kids need glasses that can keep up with them. We offer frames that are lightweight, flexible, and designed to stay comfortable throughout the day. With a range of fun styles and trusted brands, we’ll help your child find something they actually want to wear.

Lens Options That Make a Difference

The right lenses can make a big difference in how your glasses feel throughout the day. We’ll help you choose options that match your routine, whether that means reducing glare, protecting your eyes from screens, or improving clarity in bright light. It’s all about making your vision more comfortable, not more complicated.

  • Anti-reflective coatings to reduce glare and eye strain
  • Blue light protection for screen-heavy days
  • Polarized lenses for clearer vision outdoors
  • Scratch-resistant treatments for everyday durability
