Dr. Tyler Lesko, O.D.

Book AppointmentCall: (732) 978-9306

Dr. Tyler Lesko is a local eye care professional born and raised in Lacey Township, NJ. He graduated Summa Cum Laude from Georgian Court University before earning his Doctor of Optometry degree from the Pennsylvania College of Optometry (PCO), where he also graduated Summa Cum Laude.

During his doctoral studies, Dr. Lesko was a powerhouse of leadership and academic excellence. He served as the President of the Student Volunteer Optometric Services to Humanity (SVOSH) and the New Jersey Student Society of Optometric Physicians. Notably, he was the 2021 National Optometry Student Bowl Champion, outperforming students from all 23 optometry schools in the nation.

Clinical Specialties & Interests

Dr. Lesko is passionate about preserving vision through advanced technology and personalized care. His expertise includes:

  • Pediatrics & Myopia Management: Utilizing orthokeratology and MiSight to change the trajectory of children's lives.
  • Specialty Contact Lenses: Providing life-changing vision for patients through complex lens fittings.
  • Ocular Disease: Early detection and monitoring of glaucoma, macular degeneration, and cataracts.
  • Advanced Certifications: He is certified in Light Adjustable Lens technology and Orthokeratology.

Beyond the clinic, Dr. Lesko has participated in humanitarian mission trips to Panama and local vision screenings for the Special Olympics and Lions Club. At home, he enjoys spending time with his wife, son, and two cats.
